02

Active Lineups — Two-Strike Rule

Two strikes and you're out. Starting an inactive player counts as one strike. If an owner starts inactive players on two separate occasions in the same season, that owner is removed from the league.

An owner removed under the Two-Strike Rule receives no refund of league dues.
03

Keepers

Each team may keep one player per season. Keeper cost is one round earlier than the player's draft position in the immediately preceding season.

EXAMPLEA player drafted in Round 5 can be kept the next year for a Round 4 pick.

A keeper placed on the draft board counts as being drafted at that keeper position. The new draft position becomes the basis for the following season's keeper cost.

MULTI-YEAR EXAMPLE2026: drafted in the 5th → 2027: kept for a 4th → 2028: kept for a 3rd → 2029: kept for a 2nd.

First-Round Players

Players drafted or kept in the 1st round are not eligible to be kept the following season. There is no 1st-round keeper option.

Required Draft Pick

If an owner does not possess a pick in the required keeper round, the cost moves to the next earlier round until an eligible pick is available. If no eligible pick exists before reaching Round 1, the player cannot be kept.

If an owner has multiple picks in the keeper round, the owner's original draft-position pick is used. If that pick was traded, the earliest pick owned in that round is used.

Traded, Dropped & Waiver Players

Keeper eligibility follows the player. A traded player retains his draft position. A drafted player who is dropped and later added—by any team—also retains his original draft position for keeper purposes. A player who was not drafted that season may be kept the following season for the cost of a last-round pick.

Keeper Deadline & Lock

Keepers must be declared two weeks before the league draft. Once the deadline passes, all keeper declarations are final.

Injury, suspension, retirement, roster-status changes, or any other pre-draft disaster do not reopen the keeper decision. That sucks. He's still your keeper.
04

Draft Order

Draft order is based on final playoff and consolation results, not regular-season standings.

To discourage tanking, the top three picks are awarded to the 10th-, 11th-, and 12th-place finishers—in that order. The remaining picks then proceed in reverse finishing order from 9th through 1st.

10th11th12th9th8th7th6th5th4th3rd2nd1st

That produces picks 1.01 through 1.12 respectively.

05

Trades & Draft Picks

Trades process without league or commissioner approval. There is no veto or review process. Owners are responsible for evaluating and accepting their own trades.

A trade is not reversed merely because it is unfair, lopsided, stupid, or regretted later.

Future Picks

Any owner may trade draft picks up to three future drafts in advance. There are no league-imposed limits on which rounds or how many picks may be traded, subject to Sleeper platform limitations.

Trading away the pick normally needed for a keeper does not reduce the keeper's cost. The cost moves to the next earlier available round under the keeper rules.

06

Vacant Teams

If an owner leaves the league for any reason, the orphaned team is offered first to existing co-managers.

If no co-manager takes over, the opening is offered first come, first served from the waitlist, subject to commissioner approval. The commissioner retains final approval over all new league members.
